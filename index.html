🎯 Bottom Line

India remains a structurally attractive economy, but the next three months are primarily a battle between strong domestic fundamentals and hostile global liquidity conditions.

The base case is a gradual recovery toward 24,500–24,800, provided crude does not surge, US yields stabilize, the rupee remains orderly and Q2 earnings confirm the FY27 recovery.

The most important downside level is 22,200. A sustained break below it would materially weaken the thesis. Conversely, a sustained move above 23,400 would provide much stronger technical confirmation of a medium-term reversal.
