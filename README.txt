8. MAJOR RISKS
============================================================

RISK 1 — CRUDE OIL
------------------------------------------------------------

India imports a large majority of its crude oil requirements.

Higher crude can cause:

- Higher inflation
- Larger current-account pressure
- Rupee weakness
- Lower corporate margins
- RBI tightening
- FII selling

RISK 2 — US TREASURY YIELDS
------------------------------------------------------------

High US yields make US fixed income more attractive relative to
emerging-market assets.

Potential consequences:

- Stronger USD
- FII outflows
- Pressure on Indian valuations
- Rupee depreciation

RISK 3 — RUPEE
------------------------------------------------------------

A significant USD/INR depreciation can increase imported inflation
and make foreign investors more cautious.

RISK 4 — RBI
------------------------------------------------------------

A modest 25 bp hike may be manageable.

A larger-than-expected hike accompanied by hawkish forward guidance
would increase downside risk.

RISK 5 — INDIA-US TRADE POLICY
------------------------------------------------------------

Tariffs and trade negotiations can affect:

- Exporters
- IT
- Manufacturing
- Chemicals
- Engineering companies
- Currency sentiment

RISK 6 — TECHNICAL BREAKDOWN
------------------------------------------------------------

A sustained Nifty break below 22,200 would materially weaken
the accumulation / recovery thesis.

11. INVESTMENT INTERPRETATION
============================================================

The central investment thesis can be summarized as:

    STRONG DOMESTIC ECONOMY
              +
    LOWER MARKET VALUATIONS
              +
    IMPROVING EARNINGS
              -
    HIGH GLOBAL MACRO RISK
              =
    MODERATELY BULLISH / HIGH VOLATILITY

The dashboard therefore does NOT assume a straight-line rally.

The preferred strategy represented by the analysis is:

    BUY WEAKNESS RATHER THAN CHASE STRENGTH

A move toward 22,200–22,500 without a deterioration in crude,
US yields or earnings could represent a more attractive
risk/reward opportunity.

A rapid move toward 23,000–23,400 should be evaluated against
the possibility of technical resistance.
